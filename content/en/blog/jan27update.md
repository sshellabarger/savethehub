Dear Hub Community,

Thank you again for the tremendous response to our relaunch efforts. Here's where we stand and what's next:

## Progress Update

### Volunteer Response: 
25 people have signed up to help with the relaunch - an incredible show of community support!  I hope to schedule a volunteer meeting this week to assign committee/working group leads, define specific tasks and responsibilities weather permitting. I'll send a separate email with the specific date, time, and location once confirmed.The volunteer signup form remains active for anyone who wants to join: https://forms.gle/L8pVk9UEYDmHGPth9

### Property Acquisition: 
On Friday, I submitted a formal purchase offer of $1,150,000 plus assumption of the $1 million EDA mortgage to Colliers for the current Innovation Hub building.

### Alternative Location: 
We're also exploring Fire Station No. 1 in Argenta (723 Maple St, North Little Rock, AR 72114) as a potential location. The station's firefighters are scheduled to move to a new facility in the coming months. We're working with the city to learn more about their plans for the property once vacated.

### Media Coverage: 
On Monday, the Arkansas Democrat-Gazette published an article about the Hub being for sale, the EDA lien, and our group's readiness to purchase and take over operations. You can read it here: https://edition.arkansasonline.com/article/281805700344300

This public visibility helps demonstrate strong community support and our commitment to preserving the Hub's mission and the federal investment it represents.

## Stay Informed

All project documentation, financial models, and updates continue to evolve on our GitHub repository at savethehub.org. Check back regularly as we refine plans based on community input and new developments.

Thank you for your continued commitment to rebuilding Arkansas's maker community. Your involvement is making this cooperative vision a reality.
